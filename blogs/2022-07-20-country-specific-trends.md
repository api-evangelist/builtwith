You can now see country specific trends for technologies at a glance by clicking the yellow button link on the homepage of https://trends.builtwith.com - it should be default geolocate to your home country. These new pages let you quickly see what the top technologies across different technology categories are near you. This is quite useful because for example the most popular eCommerce technologies in France differ from other locations like the United States, United Kingdom and Australia.
