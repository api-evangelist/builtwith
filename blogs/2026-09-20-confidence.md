Every technology we detect on a website is given a Confidence Score from 0 to 1 — or 0% to 100% — along with a simple Low, Medium or High confidence rating. This tells you how confident we are that a detection is accurate and current. A High score means we're confident in the detection, while Medium or Low means there are reasons to treat it with more caution.
