Introducing BuiltWith Acquired and Funded Websites BuiltWith Acquired and Funded Websites provides a growing view of websites connected to company acquisitions and funding announcements. It brings these events together in one place, making it easier to discover companies experiencing important ownership, investment and growth milestones. Each record can include the company website, business category, location and the date the event was detected.
