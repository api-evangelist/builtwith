We are excited to introduce a brand new technology on BuiltWith called Pre 2000 Internet . This category highlights websites that were first registered before the year 2000. You can now explore the oldest surviving domains that helped shape the early web and you can trace their presence back to 1985 for the very small number of sites that were already online then.
