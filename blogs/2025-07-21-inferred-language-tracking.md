We’re excited to announce a powerful new enhancement to BuiltWith: Inferred Language Tracking . Traditionally, identifying the language of a website has relied on explicit HTML indicators like the html lang="..." tag. However, many sites—especially generic TLDs like .com—don’t specify their language at all.
