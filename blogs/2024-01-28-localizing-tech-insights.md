This new feature allows users to explore the technologies and companies that predominantly target or find resonance within the same country they operate. 🌐 Imagine being able to pinpoint web agencies that tailor their expertise for local businesses or identify shipping providers whose services are exclusively devoted to a specific country. This isn't just data; it's a treasure trove of insights for anyone looking to understand or penetrate local markets with unparalleled precision.
