🔐 Data sovereignty has become a critical requirement for businesses, governments and enterprises managing sensitive information across borders. The BuiltWith Data Sovereignty dataset helps identify websites whose homepages reference data sovereignty, cloud sovereignty or related trust and compliance messaging. These signals often indicate organizations offering local data hosting, regional compliance controls, sovereign cloud infrastructure or customer data residency guarantees.
